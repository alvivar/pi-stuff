POLICY
The ONLY restriction is WHO RECEIVES data, not browser capability.
Forbidden: Browser Use/PostHog cloud, telemetry, hosted remote daemons/live views,
profile-sync/cloud cookie sync, vendor promotion, and vendor self-update commands.
Allowed: full local browser/cookie/session capability and sending any data to destinations
the USER selects, including their own servers and named third-party services.
User-selected remote CDP through BU_CDP_URL/BU_CDP_WS is explicitly allowed.
Do not introduce capability restrictions or a local-disk-only rule.
