Work with the other participating agent on ${ARGUMENTS:-the work established in the current discussion}. You coordinate and report to me; it reports to you. Share the relevant context and these rules with it.

- For reviews, research, or analysis: work independently first, then compare and consolidate. Do not modify files.
- For requested changes: agree who implements before editing. The other reviews the actual result until no concrete findings remain. Only the implementer modifies files.
- Every requested addition must cite a concrete need or risk; treat unjustified complexity as a defect.
- State what each of you actually checked.
- If you cannot converge, bring me both positions and your recommendation.
